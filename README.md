##  About Me

Also known online as **Snorty** — I'm an 19-year-old tech enthusiast from Rome, Italy, with a strong passion for **cybersecurity**, **ethical hacking**, and **programming**.

I love exploring how systems work, breaking them down, understanding their weaknesses, and learning how to make them stronger. This drive to understand *and* build is what pushes most of my projects.

This profile is also my space to share what I learn — tips, guides, experiments, and ideas related to cybersecurity and computer science. If a concept I've mastered could help someone else in the field, I turn it into content.

If you're passionate about tech, hacking, or programming — you're in the right place. Welcome to my corner of the internet.
