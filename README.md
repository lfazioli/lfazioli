
# 💻 Hi :)
![Hacker](https://github.com/lfazioli/lfazioli/blob/main/Programmatore%20a%20lavoro%20con%20codice.png)
Hi! I’m Lorenzo, also known online as Snorty. I’m an 18-year-old tech enthusiast from Rome, Italy, with a strong passion for cybersecurity, ethical hacking, and programming.<br><br>I love exploring how systems work, breaking them down, understanding their weaknesses, and learning how to make them stronger. Over the years, I’ve worked on several projects — one of the most notable being IPScan, a Raycast extension I built to speed up and simplify network scanning tasks.<br><br>This blog is my space to share what I learn: tips, guides, experiments, and ideas related to cybersecurity and computer science. Whether it’s a concept I’ve mastered or something that could help others in the field, I enjoy turning my knowledge into useful content for anyone who shares my enthusiasm.<br><br>If you’re passionate about tech, hacking, or programming, you’re in the right place. Welcome to my corner of the internet.
